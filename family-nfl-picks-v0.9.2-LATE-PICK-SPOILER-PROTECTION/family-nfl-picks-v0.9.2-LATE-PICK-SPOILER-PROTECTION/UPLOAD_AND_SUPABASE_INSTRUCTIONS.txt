Family NFL Picks v0.9.2 — Late-Pick Spoiler Protection

NO COMMISSIONER MAKE-PICK FEATURE IS INCLUDED.

WHAT v0.9.2 ADDS
- If a game has kicked off and a player has NO saved pick for that game:
  * the live score is hidden from THAT player
  * everyone else's revealed picks for that game are hidden from THAT player
  * the app does not fetch the NFL score feed on that player's phone while the missed live game is protected
- The matchup itself remains visible, so the player can still make the late pick if Yasin authorizes the existing 5-minute Late Pick override.
- Immediately after the late pick is successfully saved, the score and revealed picks become visible again.
- If the game reaches FINAL without a pick, the score may reveal because final games cannot be reopened for a late pick.

FAIRNESS
This is player-specific. A person who already made their pick sees the game normally.
Other family members are not affected.

Family NFL Picks v0.9.4 — Self Override Before Submit

WHAT CHANGED
- Yasin no longer has to submit all Week picks before fixing HIS OWN missed live game.
- Before Yasin submits the week, Commish still hides everyone else's actual picks.
- If Yasin personally missed a game that is currently live, Commish shows a special:
    Your Missed Live Game
    TEAM @ TEAM
    LIVE · SCORE HIDDEN
    Authorize My Late Pick · 5 min
- No live score is shown.
- No other player's pick is shown.

IMPORTANT FAIRNESS RULES UNCHANGED
- Yasin still cannot inspect other people's picks before submitting his own week.
- Yasin still cannot use Commissioner Review to edit/authorize other players before submitting his own week.
- This adds only a safe self-rescue path for Yasin's own missed live game.
